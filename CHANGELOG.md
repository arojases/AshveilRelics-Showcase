# Changelog

## Demo v1.1 — A Village Worth Saving

**Windows build:** [Download from itch.io](https://arojases.itch.io/ashveil-relics)

### Added
- Three interactive narrative NPCs in the village using existing Pixel Crawler characters: **Citizen_F** (villager woman), **Knight** (guard), and **Wizzard** (wizard).
- Each character has up to three repeatable dialogue lines that explain the danger to the village and the history of the dungeon.
- A mission-gated underground cave entrance using existing Pixel Crawler tiles and a wooden door.
- Quest-driven door opening animation after accepting *Echoes of Darkness*.
- Solid collision around the cave and a small interaction zone directly in front of the entrance.

### Improved
- Players enter the dungeon by pressing **E** rather than crossing an invisible scene boundary.
- Before accepting the quest, the entrance remains closed and asks players to speak to the main villager.
- After completing the story, the entrance remains closed and displays the completed-quest message.
- The cave appearance was refined to avoid a large building or protruding rock formation.

### Unchanged
- Core movement, melee combat, dodge, enemy encounters, two-phase final boss, music, victory, and story epilogue.

## Demo v1.0 — Initial playable release

- Complete playable story from village quest to final boss and narrative ending.
- Skeleton dungeon encounters and orc ambush.
- Two-phase Lucifer Skeleton Ancient boss fight.
- Combat, dodge, health, retry, main menu, and audiovisual presentation.

**Note:** This is a technical documentation showcase. The full Unity project and licensed third-party asset files remain private.
